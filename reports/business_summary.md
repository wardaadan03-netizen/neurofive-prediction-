# Business Summary

Customer churn analysis helps identify customers who are at risk of leaving a telecom service.

The analysis shows that customers with month-to-month contracts, shorter tenure, and higher monthly charges have a higher probability of churn.

Machine learning models were developed using Decision Tree and Logistic Regression algorithms to predict potential churn customers.

By identifying high-risk customers early, businesses can provide personalized offers, improve customer satisfaction, and increase retention.

This predictive approach can help reduce revenue loss caused by customer churn.
